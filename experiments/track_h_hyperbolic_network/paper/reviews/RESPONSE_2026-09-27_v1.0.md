# Response to the review of v1.0

**Review:** `REVIEW_2026-09-27_v1.0.md` (verbatim). **Revised version:** v1.1.
**Method of response:** every computation the reviewer asked for was preregistered before it was run
(`../../PREREGISTRATION_3.md`, committed at 389a6a1), with rival forms and refutation criteria. One of our own

We thank the reviewer. The four substantive recommendations are addressed in turn; the typographical points are
addressed last.

## 1. Flat-lattice scaling beyond double precision (major critique A, recommendation 1)

**Agreed and done.** We compute κ for the float64-singular flat instances in 512-bit ball arithmetic (Arb via
python-flint), with certified error radii, from the integer Laplacian; the Gram matrix J^T J is formed in closed form
so J is never materialised (new §2.4). Two unsaturated controls reproduce the float64 values to better than 1e-6 in
log₁₀κ (square R=10: 9.7178; triangular R=6.45: 8.6724), with radii of order 1e-119.

The rival forms and their extrapolations were fixed before the run from the unsaturated data alone:
exp(c√N) predicts log₁₀κ = 16.42 (square N=797), 15.63 and 25.94 (triangular N=421, 1069); a power law predicts
12.3–13.9, 12.5 and 16.0.

**Result (§3.1, Table 1 last column, Fig. 1):** triangular N=421: log₁₀κ = 15.99; square N=797: 16.54 (certified
relative radii < 1e-90). Both are within 0.4 decades of the depth-mechanism extrapolation and ≥ 2.7 decades from
the power law, and the local exponent keeps increasing (8.6 → 16.4; 10.4 → 17.0), which a power law forbids. The
flat-lattice κ is exponential in √N over the tested range; limitation (i) is resolved. The largest instance,
triangular N=1069 (3071 unknowns), gives 26.95 (radius 1e-54): one decade from the depth-form extrapolation 25.94,
eleven from the power law's 15.98; local exponent 16.4 → 27.1.

## 2. Unit-conductance assumption (major critique B, recommendation 2)

**Agreed and done** (new §3.5, Table 4). We report the condition number of the log-parametrised Jacobian
∂Λ/∂ln g (relative perturbations), medians over five seeds:
- g ~ U[0.5, 1.5]: gap at N≈316 is 6.0 decades (unit: 6.4); hyperbolic κ moves by 0.25 decades.
- log-uniform over two decades [0.1, 10]: both κ rise (hyperbolic +1.9, square +3.7 decades); the gap *widens* to
  8.3 decades (3.6 at N≈112).
- ×100 and ×0.01 defects on all edges of a node at maximal depth: hyperbolic κ rises by 1.2 and 1.5 decades; the
  gap stays at 6.3–6.4.

All three preregistered predictions held (thresholds: gap ≥ 5, ≥ 3, and hyperbolic change < 2 decades).

## 3. Dimensional disparity in the probe-matched control (major critique C, recommendation 3)

**Agreed, and the reviewer's concern turned out to be right.** We preregistered the comparison the reviewer
implies: the square lattice's *best*-conditioned r-dimensional parameter subspace (top-r right singular vectors,
σ₁/σ_r) with r the hyperbolic identifiable dimension. Our prediction was that the flat value would still exceed the
hyperbolic one by 1–4 decades. It does not. At N≈112 (r=117) the flat subspace is *better* conditioned
(10^2.14 vs 10^2.70); at N≈316 (r=306) the hyperbolic side is better by only 0.5 decades (10^2.97 vs 10^3.45).

v1.1 therefore withdraws the v1.0 sentence "the conditioning advantage survives probe matching and is not a
probe-count artefact" and replaces it with the dimension-matched result (§3.3, new Table 3, limitation (iv),
abstract). The full-boundary result is unchanged, and we explain why the two are consistent: a boundary that
holds a fixed fraction of the nodes *is* the geometric property, and subsampling it removes exactly that property.
The ledger records the correction against the v1.0 claim (H0-X-0003 → H0-X-0005) without editing the original
statement.

## 4. Proposition 1's "argued, not machine-checked" step (recommendation 4)

**Done** (§3.6). The argument now given: (i) the layer truncation is a closed topological disk, certified per
instance by the Euler characteristic V − E + F = 1 that the builder already checks; (ii) every vertex lies on
exactly three heptagons covering a neighbourhood of it, so if all three are present its three edges are present
and it is off the outer face, while if one is missing the missing sector belongs to the connected complement, i.e.
the outer face. Hence interior ⇒ degree three. Nesting and exhaustion follow directly. The stronger statement
int(G_L) = G_{L−1}, which the proposition does not need, remains certified only for L ≤ 6 and is labelled so.
