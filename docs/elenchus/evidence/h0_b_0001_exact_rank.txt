Exact-arithmetic evidence for ledger claim H0-B-0001 (Tier B: exact
identity verified on concrete instances, no floating point, with the
controls Elenchus requires).

Source: experiments/track_h_hyperbolic_network/hyperbolic_exact.py
(--self-test: 6/6 checks pass, including a cross-check of the modular rank
against float64's trusted small-case rank on {7,3} L=1, 42/42, agreeing
across two independent primes).

Method: with unit conductances the Laplacian L is an integer matrix. For a
prime p with det(L_ii) != 0 mod p, the boundary-to-bulk Jacobian J is
p-integral by the same harmonic-extension construction used in
hyperbolic_network.jacobian() (dLambda/dg_e = (P_a-P_b)^T(P_a-P_b), carried
out over the finite field GF(p) instead of R), and
rank_Q(J) >= rank_{GF(p)}(J mod p): a full-rank result mod p is an EXACT
CERTIFICATE of full rational rank for that instance, not a measurement.
Agreement across two independent primes (2^31-1 and 998244353) is required
before a result is reported as certified rather than possibly prime-
specific.

Results:
- {7,3} L=1 (N=35, E=42): rank 42 mod both primes. FULL RANK, CERTIFIED.
  (Cross-checks float64's rank 42/42 on this small, unsaturated case.)
- {7,3} L=2 (N=112, E=140): rank 140 mod both primes. FULL RANK, CERTIFIED.
- square N=113 (E=200): rank 200 mod both primes. FULL RANK, CERTIFIED.
  (float64 also reported 200/200 here -- agreement, as expected at a size
  well away from any precision floor.)
- triangular N=187 (E=502): rank 502 mod both primes. FULL RANK, CERTIFIED.
- triangular N=475 (E=1331): rank 1331 mod both primes. FULL RANK,
  CERTIFIED.

The last result is the decisive one. This graph is comparable in size to
the triangular N=421 instance for which float64 (in the original,
now-corrected H0-X-0001 run) reported a rank deficiency of 102/1176
edges -- interpreted there as "structurally unrecoverable" interior
conductances. This exact result PROVES that interpretation wrong for this
family and this size range: the matrix IS exactly full rank; the float64
"deficiency" was a numerical-precision artefact of the SVD-based rank
tolerance, not a genuine algebraic property, exactly analogous to (but
distinct from) the kappa-capping bug corrected in H0-X-0002.

Consequence for the hypothesis (H1), stated plainly: H1's real content is
NOT a difference in structural identifiability between hyperbolic and flat
boundary-to-bulk inverse problems -- at the sizes tested here, BOTH
families are exactly full rank. H1's real content is a difference in
CONDITIONING: the precision required to actually exploit that full rank
grows polynomially with N for the hyperbolic case (H0-X-0002) and becomes
astronomically, super-polynomially large for the flat case, to the point
of exceeding float64's representable dynamic range well before N~1000.
Any prose (in RESULTS.md, the ledger, or a future manuscript) using
"structurally unrecoverable" or "inrecouvrable" for the flat-lattice case
is retracted by this entry and should be replaced with language about
conditioning / required precision, not identifiability.

Not yet certified: larger flat-lattice instances (N > 475 was not run
here, for runtime reasons -- Gaussian elimination over GF(p) on a
~3500x1300 matrix is the practical limit reached in this session). Full-
rank at N=475 does not by itself imply full rank at all larger N; it is
evidence against a genuine structural deficiency appearing at the same
scale where float64 first reported one, which is the specific claim this
entry retracts.
