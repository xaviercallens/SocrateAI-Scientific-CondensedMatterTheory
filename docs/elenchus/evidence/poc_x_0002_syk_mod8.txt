Claim tested: for the 4-body Majorana SYK model, the exact-degeneracy rate of
the ground state (within a fixed fermion-parity sector) depends on N mod 8.
Motivated by, but not derived from: Fidkowski-Kitaev (arXiv:0904.2197, in
this repo's corpus, `poc` pillar) -- the free Majorana chain's Z invariant
collapses to Z8 under interactions -- and You-Ludwig-Xu (arXiv:1602.06964,
same pillar) -- SYK's level statistics, as the boundary theory of a
many-body-localized 1D SPT phase, cycle through the Wigner-Dyson ensembles
with a period tied to that topological index. Neither paper's symmetry
operator was constructed or verified here; this is an empirical test of the
resulting qualitative pattern only.

Method: Majorana operators built as signed permutations of the N/2-qubit
computational basis (Jordan-Wigner), self-tested against a brute-force dense
Kronecker-product construction on N=4 and against {gamma_i,gamma_j}=2*delta_ij
and [H,P]=0 on N=6/N=8 before any physics run (`syk_entanglement.py
--self-test`, 8/8 checks pass). Couplings: i.i.d. Gaussian J_ijkl, variance
6/N^3 (J=1 unit of energy, Maldacena-Stanford 1604.07818 normalization up to
the overall constant). Ground state identified within its own fermion-parity
sector (parity purity checked, not assumed); a realization whose same-sector
gap to the next level is below 1e-8 of the energy scale is flagged
"degenerate" rather than having its entanglement spectrum computed from an
arbitrarily-chosen vector in a near-degenerate subspace.

Result, N=8  (N mod 8 = 0): 0/40 realizations degenerate.
Result, N=12 (N mod 8 = 4): 40/40 realizations degenerate.
Result, N=16 (N mod 8 = 0): 0/40 realizations degenerate.
Result, N=20 (N mod 8 = 4): 40/40 realizations degenerate.
Result, N=24 (N mod 8 = 0): 0/40 realizations degenerate.

Perfect all-or-nothing split at every tested N, not merely "more frequent":
same-sector gap / energy scale is ~1e-15 (machine precision) for every
N mod 8 = 4 realization and ~1e-4 to 1e0 (a real physical gap) for every
N mod 8 = 0 realization -- roughly 14 orders of magnitude of separation,
visible directly in the raw per-realization scatter (figures/syk_degeneracy.
png, middle panel), which is the actual evidence; the persistence panel
(rightmost) reproduces the same separation UNSUPERVISED, run on all 200
realizations pooled with no N-mod-8 label given to Gudhi, and returns one H0
bar of length 10.7 decades far above all others.

Explicitly NOT established by this evidence: the antiunitary symmetry
operator behind the pattern (not constructed); generality beyond N <= 24 or
beyond the N mod 8 in {0, 4} classes reachable with N % 4 == 0 (N mod 8 in
{2, 6} were not tested, they would need an odd number of qubit pairs and a
different bipartition); any claim that this is the same kind of topological
invariant as the winding number nu proved for SSH (SSH-B-0001/0002) -- there,
an exact quantity is proved in exact arithmetic; here, a pattern is measured
in floating point over a small sample of N values, and is reported as such.
