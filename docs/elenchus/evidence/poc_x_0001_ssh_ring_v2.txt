Corrected claim, on an SSH ring of L = 200 cells (h(z) = v + wz, w = 1,
r = v/w in {0.5, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9}), subsystem of ell whole cells (both cut
bonds w-type):

 S1  The two levels closest to 1/2 are a chiral pair, z1 + z2 = 1, to 1e-15
     for every (r, ell).  [pass, 4/4]
 S2  At ell = L/2 exactly, |z - 1/2| < 3e-15: the two edge-like modes' hybrid-
     isation is cancelled EXACTLY by the ring's reflection through the two cut
     points combined with chiral symmetry. This is the geometry version 1
     tested (at two ring sizes), which is why it saw no smearing.  [pass, 4/4]
 S3  |z - 1/2| is symmetric under ell -> L - ell (tol 1e-6 rel + 1e-14 abs,
     the floating-point noise floor).  [pass, 4/4]
 S4  For ell < L/2, |z - 1/2| decays exponentially in ell with rate ln r:
     fitted xi = 1.39 / 2.68 / 4.23 / 8.42 cells vs theory -1/ln r =
     1.44 / 2.80 / 4.48 / 9.49 (within 15%); successive ratios all < 1 with
     spread <= 0.014 (compensated flatness).  [pass, 8/8]

So the preregistered sub-prediction (smearing of width ~xi that shrinks as
the system grows) was correct; version 1's "refutation" was an artefact of
testing only the symmetric cut.

Design result for the tank (D1, open chain with one domain wall, 30 cells
per side, both ends terminated on strong bonds, odd site count):
 - the wall mode has energy exactly 0 (< 5e-16) by chiral symmetry;
 - it peaks at the wall site, not at a chain end (asserted);
 - its profile decays with xi = 1.44 / 2.80 / 4.48 / 9.49 cells (matches
   theory to 3 digits);
 - 99% of its weight lies within 3 / 6 / 10 / 21 cells per side.
 A first version of this construction had the strong/weak labels backwards
 (both ends weak -> end modes -> fitted xi < 0 with the correct magnitude);
 the bond pattern is now asserted in code rather than trusted.

How the version-1 error was found: an external reviewer (not this session)
asked "what about ell != L/2?". Producer != verifier did the work; the
result was the one wanted, which is when Elenchus says to be most suspicious.
